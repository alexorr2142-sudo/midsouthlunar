# Peer team feedback (Tuesday item 3 and Thursday item 3)

Another project team tests the live site on their own phones and laptops and answers a short Google Form. Alex creates the form from the question list below and shares the link. Responses are copied into the log at the bottom and the changes we made are recorded next to them.

## Google Form: questions to create

**Title:** Mid-South Lunar New Year website, peer test

**Intro text:** Open https://alexorr2142-sudo.github.io/midsouthlunar/ on your phone if you can. Try each task below, then tell us what happened. Be blunt, it helps.

1. Your team name (short answer)
2. What device and browser did you use? (short answer)
3. **Task 1:** Find the festival dates and the venue address. Did you find them? (Yes easily / Yes with effort / No)
4. **Task 2:** Find out what is happening on Saturday Feb 6 on the Main Stage. (Yes easily / Yes with effort / No)
5. **Task 3:** Find a vendor that sells dumplings. (Yes easily / Yes with effort / No)
6. **Task 4:** Get to the ticket purchase page. (Yes easily / Yes with effort / No)
7. **Task 5:** Find where to sign up as a volunteer. (Yes easily / Yes with effort / No)
8. **Task 6:** Switch the site to Chinese and back. (Yes easily / Yes with effort / No)
9. How did the site feel on a phone? (Great / Fine / Awkward / Broken) plus a comment box
10. Did anything look wrong, confusing, or broken? (paragraph)
11. What is one thing you would change first? (paragraph)
12. Rate the overall design 1 to 5 (linear scale)

## For the Thursday round, add:

## How to run the session (about 10 minutes)

1. Share the site link and the form link with the other team.
2. Do not help them. Watch where they hesitate and note it, even if they do not report it.
3. After they submit, ask the two open questions out loud: "What was the most confusing moment?" and "What would stop you from using this at the festival?"
4. Copy every response into the log above, decide what to change, and link the commit.
